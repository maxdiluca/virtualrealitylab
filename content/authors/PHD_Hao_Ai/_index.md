Hao Ai is a PhD candidate in the School of Computer Science at the University of Birmingham, supervised by Eyal Ofek, Aleš Leonardis and Jianbo Jiao. His research concerns scene understanding, scene generation and 3D geometry estimation, with a current focus on human-centred interactive reconstruction of the physical world.

Further information is available on his [personal academic website](https://haoai-1997.github.io/) and the [MIx research group website](https://mix.jianbojiao.com/people/).
