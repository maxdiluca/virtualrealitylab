The **Virtual Reality Lab** in the School of Psychology at the University of Birmingham was inaugurated on **15 September 2023**.

The event marked the opening of the lab's facilities in the Gisbert Kapp Building. Attendees gathered for opening remarks and explored hands-on demonstrations using virtual- and mixed-reality headsets and other interactive systems.
