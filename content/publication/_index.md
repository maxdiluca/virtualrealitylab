Our publications investigate how immersive technologies shape perception, movement, interaction and collaboration. Explore recent work involving VR Lab members, including haptics, embodiment, text entry, multimodal interaction and applied immersive systems.

This collection covers selected publications involving VR Lab members from 2023 onwards, the year of the [lab's inauguration](/event/lab-inauguration/). It includes research begun before the opening and is not a complete lab bibliography. Years follow the published citation; earlier online publication dates are noted where relevant.
