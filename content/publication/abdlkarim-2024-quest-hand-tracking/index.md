abstract: This study describes a framework for measuring positional accuracy, finger joint-angle accuracy and delay
  in markerless VR hand tracking. Evaluation of the Meta Quest 2 found average fingertip position error of 1.1 cm,
  joint-angle error of 9.6 degrees and delay of 45.0 ms under the tested conditions. The paper also examines how
  tracking errors vary across the measurement space and presents a positional correction method.
