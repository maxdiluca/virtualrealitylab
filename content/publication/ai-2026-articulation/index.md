abstract: Articulation in Motion (AiM) reconstructs an articulated object from an initial scan and a video of a
  person moving its parts. A representation combining static and deformable 3D Gaussians separates motion from stationary
  geometry. Sequential RANSAC then groups moving elements into rigid parts and estimates their joints without requiring
  the number of parts in advance. Experiments on simpler and more complex objects evaluate reconstruction, segmentation
  and motion estimation. The approach is intended to produce interactive digital replicas while reducing dependence
  on structural assumptions about the object.
