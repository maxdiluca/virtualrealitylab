abstract: This paper revisits multimodal window control in extended reality using a large language model. The system
  combines scene semantics, application state, speech, pointing and head orientation to determine window-placement
  actions. Users can issue direct instructions, refer to objects through speech and gestures, or express a broader
  task goal. The contribution is a context-sensitive interaction approach for organising an immersive workspace.
