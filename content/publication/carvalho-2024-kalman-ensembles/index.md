abstract: A Kalman-filter model estimates changing phase and period correction in musical ensembles. The authors
  illustrate the approach using string-quartet recordings, examining how performers adjust timing under different
  expressive conditions. The model accommodates local tempo changes; broader comparisons with other synchronization
  models are identified as future work.
