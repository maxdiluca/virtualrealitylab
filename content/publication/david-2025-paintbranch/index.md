abstract: PaintBranch supports asynchronous collaborative painting in virtual reality through version-control features.
  A study with eight participants, arranged in four pairs, followed collaboration across a week, with four painting
  sessions per participant. The paper describes how participants used branching and how their collaboration developed,
  including ways experienced artists combined individual and shared creative aims.
