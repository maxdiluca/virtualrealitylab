abstract: The paper proposes treating extended reality as an interface through which people can inspect, modify,
  authorise and interrupt robot actions in their immediate context. Examples include nursing assistance, supervision
  of multiple robot arms and collaborative assembly. It develops four mediation functions and six design dimensions
  covering shared control, constraints, uncertainty, correction, responsibility and recovery. The contribution is
  a conceptual framework and research agenda for human–robot teaming.
