abstract: SwapSense separates a reusable camera-based force sensor from interchangeable passive haptic components.
  A neural network estimates contact force from deformation images, while attached modules change how the interface
  feels. The evaluation examines generalisation across users and adaptation to previously unseen components. A perceptual
  study also characterises five haptic augmentations. In the reported evaluation, adaptation used no more than a
  quarter of the target component’s data and less than 15 minutes of fine-tuning.
