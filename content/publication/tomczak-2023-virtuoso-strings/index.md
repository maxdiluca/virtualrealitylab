abstract: Virtuoso Strings provides multitrack string-ensemble recordings with note-onset annotations for studying
  performance timing and automatic music transcription. The dataset includes quartet, trio, duet and solo performances
  with different expressive instructions and leadership roles. It contains 746 tracks and 68,728 annotated onsets,
  with annotations prepared and checked using a semi-automatic workflow.
