abstract: MagicPen changes its length while remaining in contact with a physical surface, allowing a stylus to provide
  grounded feedback during VR modelling and drawing. A compliant mode follows geometry being edited, whereas a rigid
  mode constrains movement to an existing virtual surface. Three studies compare it with mid-air interaction in
  depth positioning and drawing tasks. The studies report improved depth positioning and support for perceiving
  geometry through surface contact.
