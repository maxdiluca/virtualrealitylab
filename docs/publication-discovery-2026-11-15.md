# Publication discovery review — 15 November 2026

All 31 publication records were searched by title or distinctive title phrase. Author, publisher, institutional, conference and project sources were used to verify candidate resources. This is a bounded resource search, not an exhaustive availability claim.

## Result

- 20 new links across 16 papers; existing labels clarified for Kalman ensembles, biological kinematics and UAV uncertainty.
- 9 publication entries contain 11 highlighted author occurrences for 6 verified current or former lab students. Seven people are registered, including Arindam Dutta, who does not appear in the current archive. Labels describe current or former lab studentship, not necessarily affiliation during the study.
- All 17 newly added external destinations passed HTTP checks; YouTube videos also returned matching oEmbed titles. Three new internal links are checked against the generated site.
- Direct author-hosted MP4 links returned HTTP 403. An author-linked MagicPen PDF returned 404, and a GroundedReach YouTube candidate returned Not Found. These were not added.
