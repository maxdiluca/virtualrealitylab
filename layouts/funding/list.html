Amounts and how the sizes are calculated

Every circle has the same base area, plus an additional area proportional to contribution value. This keeps smaller contributors visible while preserving the order of funding amounts. Total circle areas are therefore not strictly proportional to funding. Recorded amounts and estimates are listed below.

Values combine recorded cash awards, source-recorded approximate values and estimated costs of donated equipment. Estimates use cited prices, quantities and explicit assumptions; they are not invoices or audited values. Agreed donations can be included before delivery and are identified as such. Birmingham allocations are used where available; awards held by other institutions and unawarded applications are excluded. Each contribution is counted once under its funder, including AHRC support delivered through CreaTech Frontiers to MyJAMS.

Euro and US-dollar amounts are converted to pounds using ECB reference rates of 6 November 2026 (£0.83415 and US$1.1165 per euro). This provides a common visual scale, not historical accounting values. Approximate source amounts remain approximate. Unknown amounts are omitted, so these sizes do not represent each supporter’s complete contribution.
