The lab is on the fourth floor of Gisbert Kapp, entered through 52 Pritchatts Road, across the road from the University North Gate.

Find Gisbert Kapp on the campus map

  1. Enter through the courtyard entrance shown in the photograph.
  2. Facing Café 52, take the lifts on the left to the fourth floor.
  3. Turn left when leaving the lift and go through the door into the corridor. The lab is at the end of the corridor.

Corridor access requires a University ID card. Contact us before your visit if you need someone to meet you at the lift.

Visitor parking is possible at the nearby North East multi-storey car park.
