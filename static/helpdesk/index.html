VR Lab AI helpdesk

Practical guidance for approved members of the University of Birmingham Virtual Reality Lab.

Ask a clear question

Email vrlabbham@gmail.com with one clear question about the lab’s general guidance, forms or procedures. The service checks authenticated email and current lab membership before answering. A shared reply is withheld if any requester or CC recipient is ineligible. For human help, write directly to m.diluca@bham.ac.uk; the Gmail address is the AI helpdesk.

How answers are prepared

The helpdesk runs on a lab-operated computer. It uses a local AI model and selected lab guidance to prepare source-supported answers. Replies identify the use of AI and provide source links where available. AI answers can be incomplete or mistaken; check the cited guidance and any requirements for your particular activity.

Limits and human help

A helpdesk answer does not grant room access, certify induction or training, approve research or expenditure, make a booking, or authorise an activity. Sensitive, unsafe, unsupported or ambiguous questions may be withheld. Automatic referrals to a human reviewer are currently disabled. Contact m.diluca@bham.ac.uk directly when human help is needed.

Lab use only

This service is operated for approved lab members. It is not a public sign-up service and does not connect to requesters’ own mailboxes. Only the designated helpdesk mailbox is connected to the installed worker. Lab membership is checked against the current owner-selected SharePoint Lab Members List and fresh authenticated enrolment evidence, with date and freshness limits.
