Helpdesk privacy notice

This notice explains the Google mailbox data used by the VR Lab AI helpdesk.

Purpose and operator

The lab operates the helpdesk to answer eligible members’ general procedural questions. The connected account is vrlabbham@gmail.com. For questions about this service or its data handling, contact m.diluca@bham.ac.uk.

Information used

The worker reads messages in the designated helpdesk mailbox, including sender and recipient headers, the subject, message text and thread identifiers. It uses these to screen unsafe instructions, verify authenticated senders, identify the current question, prevent duplicate replies and check the intended recipients. It does not open email attachments or follow links supplied in an enquiry. A separate protected membership record contains email addresses, recorded roles and eligibility dates or source-event verification.

Google permissions

The application requests Gmail read-only access and permission to send mail from the connected helpdesk account. It does not request permission to delete messages, change mailbox labels or manage users’ accounts. The installed worker verifies the connected mailbox’s identity before processing messages. Requesters do not grant it access to their own mailboxes.

Local AI and source guidance

Eligible enquiries are reduced to the current question and redacted where the processing rules require it. The local AI model receives that question, selected source guidance and controlled sender context, which can include the sender’s email address, recorded roles and a recorded name if one is present. These fields are context, not permission to perform an action. The helpdesk does not call a cloud AI service for answer generation and does not train a model on enquiries. Google still processes messages as the mailbox provider; Microsoft hosts the source guidance and membership list. Local AI processing does not move the Gmail mailbox or the SharePoint source systems onto the lab computer.

Replies and operator copies

Replies go to the authenticated requester and eligible original CC recipients. A redacted question and the answer may be sent to the sole lab operator as an observation copy. The service does not sell mailbox data or use it for advertising. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Original enquiries and sent replies remain in the Gmail mailbox under the mailbox owner’s retention arrangements. Private local state holds membership information, duplicate-prevention records and temporary delivery-recovery information. Recovery mappings are bounded to fourteen days; opaque records can remain to prevent repeat sends and support audit. This service does not automatically delete mailbox messages. Access to local credentials and operational records is restricted to the computer’s owner account.

Choice and requests

Do not email passwords, access codes, participant data, confidential personnel records or other sensitive case material. Ask the operator for the appropriate route instead. Contact m.diluca@bham.ac.uk about correcting membership information or handling an enquiry record. The mailbox owner can revoke the application’s Google access; the helpdesk will then stop until it is reconnected.
