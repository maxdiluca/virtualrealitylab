Using the helpdesk

Guidance on the intended use and limits of the VR Lab AI helpdesk.

Who the service is for

The service is for approved lab members whose authenticated email and current eligibility pass the helpdesk’s checks. Sending an enquiry does not create membership or grant access. If the record is missing, expired or unclear, contact the lab operator directly.

What to send

Ask one clear question about general lab guidance, forms or procedures. Keep personal and sensitive details to the minimum needed. Do not send passwords, door codes, confidential records or participant data.

How to use an answer

Use the reply as a starting point and follow its cited sources. A reply is not approval, a completed booking, a safety release, an ethics decision or confirmation that access has been activated. Those outcomes must be confirmed by their responsible owners and systems.

When an answer is withheld

The service can withhold a reply because of authentication, membership, security, safety, source, uncertainty or delivery checks. Do not rely on it for urgent assistance. Human help is available by contacting m.diluca@bham.ac.uk directly; automated human-review referrals are disabled.

Service availability

The helpdesk depends on the lab computer being logged in, awake and connected, as well as Gmail, current membership evidence and the local AI service. It may be unavailable while those requirements are unmet.
